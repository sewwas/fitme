Official Fit Me Club Rules & Etiquette

To ensure an exceptional training environment, all members agree to uphold our facility standards:

Indoor Footwear Only
Outdoor shoes strictly prohibited. Members must bring a separate pair of clean indoor workout shoes.
Mandatory Workout Towel
Use of a small towel during workouts is strictly mandatory for equipment hygiene and sanitation.
100% Substance Free
Smoking and the possession or use of drugs are strictly prohibited throughout the club premises.
Hydration Permitted
Bringing food into workout zones is prohibited. Water, isotonic sports drinks, and protein shakes are allowed.
