Frequently Asked Questions & Rules

Key standards, turnstile speed gate rules, and facility etiquette.

What is the footwear and towel policy?
Outdoor shoes are strictly prohibited on the workout floor. All members must bring a separate pair of clean indoor-only training shoes. Bringing a personal sweat towel is mandatory for hygiene and sanitation.
How do the biometric speed gates work?
Once your membership is approved and paid at the front desk, you are issued a unique 4-digit hardware PIN (e.g. #1001) and digital QR pass. Enter your PIN or scan your pass at the turnstile keypad to enter automatically.
Are personal trainers included?
General floor supervision and onboarding fitness assessments are included with all memberships. For dedicated 1-on-1 personal training, you can enroll in our 1-on-1 Personal Coaching program.
Where is Fit Me located and what are the hours?
We are located at New Town, Elpitiya Road, Pitigala, 80420. Operating hours: Monday – Friday 05:30 AM – 11:00 PM, Saturday – Sunday 06:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Automated biometric gates are active 24/7 for authorized members.
